Privacy policy

Kiri Arcade: Multigame ("the app") is made by Kiri Cluster ("we"), based in Venezuela. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in. Your progress stays on your device.
  • The app has no analytics or crash reporting tools of its own, and we have no servers that receive your data. Google's ads SDK may collect diagnostic and usage data on its own (see Ads).
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect some data from your device to show and measure ads. You can choose your ad and data options, and change them later in Settings > Privacy options (when shown).

Data stored on your device

The app saves your progress locally on your device: Cyber Credits, KIRO's items and mood, game scores, statistics and saved rounds, your settings (language, theme, vibration, reduce motion), the install date (the app uses it to show no ads during the first minutes), and the times of the last gift, rest reminder and ad. We never receive this data. To delete it, use Settings > Help and data > Delete my data (it asks for confirmation and cannot be undone), uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android's settings. Deleting it from the app does not change your ad choices; use Privacy options for those.

Android may keep a copy of your progress in your Google account if you have backups turned on. That copy is managed by Google and your device settings; the app does not receive it or read it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK by Google. When ads are allowed, the SDK may collect and share with Google, according to Google's own disclosure:

  • your device's IP address (which may be used to estimate general location);
  • device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID);
  • interactions with ads and the app (app launch, taps, video views);
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance.

Google uses this data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. See how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The app requests ads suitable for teens (maximum content rating "T") and does not treat users as children.

Consent and privacy options

Before any ad is requested, the app asks Google whether your consent is needed where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland). If it is, Google's consent form is shown first. If your choice does not allow ads, no ads are requested. When Google requires it, Settings shows Privacy options, where you can review or change your choice at any time.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Analytics and crash reports

The app has no analytics and no crash reporting tools of its own. The Google Mobile Ads SDK it includes may, on its own, collect diagnostic and usage information (such as app and SDK performance and interactions with ads) for Google, as described in "Ads" above; it includes Google's measurement component (play-services-measurement-sdk-api) for this. Google Play may give us aggregated statistics (installs, crashes) collected by Android and Google Play under Google's policies, if you agreed to share them with developers on your device.

International transfers

Google may process ad data on servers in different countries, as its privacy policy explains.

Feedback emails

Settings > Help and data > Send feedback opens your own mail app with a draft addressed to us. The draft includes the app version, your Android version and your phone model (read from the app's web view, without any extra permission), and space for your message. You can read and change all of it. The app sends nothing by itself: we only receive the email if you decide to send it. If you do, we receive what you send and your email address, use them only to answer you and improve the app, and delete them on request.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Vibration: gentle feedback in games (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Advertising ID and related ad services permissions: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Age

The app is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, contact us and we will help (we hold no data on our side; see "Your rights").

Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you on our servers. The only exception is a feedback email you choose to send us: you can ask us to show or delete it. You can delete your local data with Settings > Help and data > Delete my data, by uninstalling the app, or by clearing its data. For data collected by Google through ads, use Privacy options in the app, your device's ad settings, or Google's tools described in its privacy policy. Depending on where you live (for example GDPR in the EU, or local data protection laws), you may have additional rights; contact us to exercise them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this address. Important changes will be noted in the app's store listing or release notes.
